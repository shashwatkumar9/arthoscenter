# Surgery Content Generation Guide

## Structure for Each Surgery

Each surgery needs:

1. **Top 5 SEO Keywords** (Examples):
   - Total Knee Replacement: "knee replacement surgery", "total knee arthroplasty", "knee replacement cost", "best knee surgeon", "knee replacement recovery"
   - ACL Reconstruction: "ACL surgery", "ACL tear treatment", "knee ligament surgery", "ACL reconstruction recovery", "sports knee injury"

2. **Content Requirements** (1500+ words per surgery):
   - Comprehensive overview (300-400 words)
   - Detailed symptoms & indications (200 words)
   - Procedure details with step-by-step (400 words)
   - Recovery timeline with weekly breakdown (300 words)
   - 8-10 FAQs covering: cost, pain, recovery time, success rate, age limits, insurance, complications
   - Related surgeries section

3. **SEO Optimization**:
   - Natural keyword placement in H1, H2, first paragraph
   - Long-tail keyword variations
   - Local SEO: "knee replacement in Bihar", "best orthopedic surgeon Patna"
   - Schema.org markup (already implemented in page template)

4. **Bilingual Requirements**:
   - Full Hindi translations for all content
   - Culturally appropriate medical terminology
   - Hindi keywords for local search
